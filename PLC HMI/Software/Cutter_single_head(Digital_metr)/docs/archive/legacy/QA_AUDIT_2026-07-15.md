## Critical Findings

1. Touch I2C can enter `ESP_ERR_INVALID_STATE` after reset/long runtime.
   - Evidence: serial logs during QSTA/FRST had repeated `i2c_master_transmit failed: [259] ESP_ERR_INVALID_STATE`.
   - Immediate mitigation added: touch read now backs off after repeated empty/failing reads to reduce serial flood and UI load.
   - Required next validation: physical touch test after power-cycle, not only software reset.

2. The old partition layout had too little NVS for 100-row cut-list persistence plus settings.
   - Evidence: factory reset produced many `NOT_ENOUGH_SPACE` errors.
   - Fix applied: `firmware/partitions_8mb_nvs64.csv` and `board_build.partitions = partitions_8mb_nvs64.csv`.
   - Required next validation: fill 100 rows, reboot, factory reset, refill 100 rows.

3. SHOT/QSTA serial logs can be polluted by low-level touch errors.
   - This makes automated parsing noisy.
   - Required next fix: make touch-driver errors silent or health-rate-limited at source if the library supports it.
